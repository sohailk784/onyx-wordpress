# Wholesale / B2B

Implemented for free: a Fluent Forms **wholesale application** page and a "Trader Login" link to My Account.

These need a paid plugin (**B2BKing** or **Wholesale Suite / Wholesale Prices Premium**):
- Wholesale customer role with approval workflow and registration fields
- Tiered / role-based pricing and quantity breaks
- Hiding retail prices or minimum order values for traders
- Tax-exempt trade accounts and invoice / net-terms payment
